# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want CodeVault-Community to be a welcoming, respectful, and constructive space for everyone, regardless of experience, background, identity, or perspective.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect and kindness.
- Assume good intent while addressing the impact of words and actions.
- Give feedback that is specific, constructive, and focused on ideas or work.
- Welcome questions from beginners.
- Respect different viewpoints and experience levels.
- Keep discussions relevant and professional.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Insults, intimidation, or deliberate humiliation.
- Sharing someone else's private information without permission.
- Spam, unwanted promotion, or repeated disruptive behavior.
- Publishing secrets or sensitive information.

## Reporting concerns

Report conduct concerns privately to the organization owner or a repository maintainer through GitHub. Avoid posting sensitive details in public issues. Reports will be reviewed as fairly and discreetly as possible.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

## Scope

This code applies to organization repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other community spaces operated by CodeVault-Community.
